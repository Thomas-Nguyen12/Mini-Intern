# A Mini-Intern for all your Mini needs 

In the age of Modern A.I., automation is everything. The versatility of Generative A.I. (GenAI), including **language models** and **Agentic A.I.**, is extremely broad with applications ranging from medicine to finance. 

Major players in the world of GenAI include ChatGPT and Claude, known as **Large Language Models** (LLM) for their high reasoning skills and parameter count: exceeding 15 billion parameters. As such, LLMs are capable of a wide range of dialogue and tasks, depending on the user's needs. 

However, LLMs, as a result of their broad skills, have high memory and power usage, with the RAM footprint for SDKs interacting with Sonnet 5 exceeding 18 - 22 GB of RAM. 

Although it is currently possible to host language models, such as Ollama, locally, they require high RAM usage and may be infeasible for smaller projects or tasks.
